Our purpose

YNAN works to protect and promote nurses and the nursing profession in Nepal.

What we stand for

A stronger profession. Better health for everyone.

Our work is guided by the needs and potential of Nepal's nursing community. These are the priorities we are working to advance together.

01

Protect the profession

Promote the dignity, rights and wellbeing of nurses, and support a safe, respectful working environment.

02

Advance nursing

Encourage learning, professional development and opportunities for nurses to lead and contribute.

03

Serve communities

Champion compassionate, equitable care and the valuable role nurses play in community health.
