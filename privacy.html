Privacy notice

This notice explains how YNAN uses the information you provide in a membership application.

What we collect

We collect your name, age or date of birth, gender, email, phone number, province, district, address, current status, college or workplace, optional council registration number, and optional reason for joining.

Why we use it

We use these details to review your membership application, contact you about it, and manage membership records.

Who can see it

Only YNAN committee members who manage membership may access application details. The application sheet is stored in Google Sheets and is shared only with those committee members.

How long we keep it

Unsuccessful applications are deleted after 12 months. For accepted members, we review the record annually and keep it while membership is active; records are deleted when they are no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

Your choices

To ask for a correction or deletion, email ynursesassociation@gmail.com.

This notice may be updated when YNAN's membership process changes.
