About YNAN

The Young Nurses Association of Nepal (YNAN) is a youth-led, non-profit nursing organization officially established and registered in 2026.

Our vision

A strong, united and respected nursing community for a healthier Nepal.

Our mission

To unite, empower and advocate for the rights, welfare and advancement of nurses in Nepal.

Our objectives

What we work toward

  1. 01

    Promote and protect the rights and welfare of young nurses.

  2. 02

    Advance professional development and nursing education.

  3. 03

    Advocate for better working conditions, fair policies and professional recognition.

  4. 04

    Contribute to public health and a stronger nursing community.

  5. 05

    Collaborate with national and international partners for shared goals.

Our activities

How we turn our mission into action

01

Advocacy & policy engagement

Amplify the voice of young nurses and advocate for rights, welfare and professional advancement at all levels.

02

Education & capacity building

Offer training, workshops, seminars and mentorship programs to enhance skills and knowledge.

03

Professional networking

Connect young nurses, practicing nurses and students to build a strong, supportive network.

04

Public health & community service

Support health campaigns, awareness programs and community outreach initiatives.

05

Collaboration & partnership

Work with national and international organizations to achieve shared goals.

06

Research & innovation

Support nursing research, evidence-based practice and innovation in healthcare.
